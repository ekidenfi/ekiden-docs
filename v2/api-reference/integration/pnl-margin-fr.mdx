This page describes the margin- and funding-related fields returned by the position endpoints.

## Position Information

Position information is available through:

```http
GET /api/v1/position/list
```

The response includes commonly used margin and risk fields such as:

- `entry_price`
- `mark_price`
- `unrealised_pnl`
- `realised_pnl`
- `margin_mode`
- `initial_margin`
- `maintenance_margin`
- `leverage`
- `liq_price`
- `funding`

---

## Margin Mode

Ekiden currently supports **Cross Margin** only.

In Cross Margin mode:

- Margin is shared across all positions within the trading account.
- Available account equity may be used to prevent liquidation.
- Liquidation risk is evaluated using the account's available margin.

The current margin mode is returned in the `margin_mode` field.

### Multi-asset collateral

When a trading account holds collateral other than USDCx, each asset counts at a weight of its value:

- **Buying power** uses the initial weight (`initial_total_collateral`). An asset gives buying power only while it is active and freshly priced.
- **Liquidation** uses the maintenance weight (`maintenance_total_collateral`, and `equity`). Collateral on hold for a withdrawal is excluded.
- **Losses and funding** are taken from USDCx, then stablecoins in `liquidation_priority` order. Volatile collateral is never spent: the rest becomes `debt`, which liquidation clears by selling volatile collateral at its `liquidation_discount`.
- **Closed markets**: while an asset's `market_session` is `closed`, it gives no buying power and counts at its frozen price less `closed_market_haircut`.

See [Account Data](/v2/api-reference/integration/account-data#balance-fields) for the balance fields and [Collateral & Margin](/v2/collateral) for the full rules.

## Risk Fields

Position responses include the following risk-related fields:

- `initial_margin`
- `maintenance_margin`
- `leverage`
- `liq_price`

These values are calculated by the trading engine and returned for informational purposes.

---

## Funding

Funding-related information is available through:

- Position responses (`funding`)
- Funding Rate History:

```http
GET /api/v1/market/funding/history
```

Refer to the Market Data documentation for funding rate history endpoints.

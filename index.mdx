Ekiden is infrastructure for tokenized derivative markets on [Canton Network](https://www.canton.network/). It provides an orderbook-based venue for perpetual futures, combining off-chain matching with deferred on-chain settlement.

Ekiden is designed for traders, market makers, and teams that need low-latency execution, transparent market structure, and programmable access to a derivatives venue.

## How Ekiden works

- **Central Limit Orderbook (CLOB)** — orders are matched off-chain for responsive price discovery and execution.
- **Deferred on-chain settlement** — batched updates are settled on Canton, balancing throughput with verifiability.
- **Merkle-based proofs** — order and position data can be verified through cryptographic commitments.
- **Self-custody** — users retain control of their assets while trading through the platform.

Read the [Architecture guide](/architecture) for a deeper explanation of Ekiden's design.

## Built for active markets

Ekiden supports perpetual futures trading with a maker–taker fee model, account and position management, and tools for active traders. Market makers and algorithmic traders can integrate through the [API Reference](/api-reference/introduction) and real-time WebSocket feeds.

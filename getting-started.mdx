This guide takes you from your first sign-in to a funded Trading Account, ready to use on the [Ekiden exchange](https://app.ekiden.fi).

<Note>
  Ekiden operates on mainnet. Depending on your access status, an access code may be required before trading becomes available.
</Note>

## Before you begin

- Use the [Ekiden app](https://app.ekiden.fi) to create or sign in to your account.
- Have your access code ready if access has not yet been activated.
- Use a source wallet and an asset or network shown as supported in the in-app deposit flow.
- Review the destination account before you submit a bridge transaction.

<Warning>
  Always confirm that the Canton recipient displayed by Ekiden belongs to the account you intend to fund. Transactions sent to a different recipient cannot be reassigned automatically.
</Warning>

## 1. Create an account or log in

Open the [Ekiden app](https://app.ekiden.fi) and select **Connect**.

Choose one of the available sign-in methods:

- **Google** is the fastest option for a new account. Your Party ID is provisioned during the sign-in flow; no browser extension is required.
- **Console Wallet** is available if you already use a Canton wallet.

## 2. Find and share your Party ID

If your account is not yet enabled, you may see a **Not on the access list** screen. Your Party ID is shown at the bottom.

1. Find **Your party ID** — it begins with ekiden-.
2. Select the copy icon.
3. Send the Party ID to your Ekiden contact or the team handling your access.

## 3. Activate access

When your account has been approved, enter the access code you receive.

1. Enter the code on the access screen.
2. Select **Activate**.
3. After activation, continue to the funding flow.

## 4. Open Deposit/Withdraw

In the app, open **Deposit/Withdraw** and select **EVM to Canton Bridge**.

1. Connect the source wallet you want to fund from.
2. Choose the source network and the asset shown in the flow.
3. Enter the amount you want to bridge.
4. Check the Canton recipient shown for your Ekiden account before continuing.

<Frame caption="Choose the source wallet, network, asset, amount, and Canton recipient">

## 5. Approve and submit the bridge transaction

Your wallet may ask you to approve the asset before the bridge can transfer it.

1. Confirm the recipient and amount again.
2. Approve the token in your wallet when prompted.
3. Submit the bridge transaction.

<Frame caption="Approve the asset in your connected wallet">
  ![Wallet approval for bridge](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXcQYK1lmW9ui3pbozIbG26nkw2dKrdcKcM_ETGo8a7Scxc2qw28WufUpKBkZ-3IrWi8yMLHi6Drkn78f8o5abzffBx-GP5fUzyljZzTluEiuqu9iZQ_jLh26L3iP1Mby_ItD8fxzuKxtUrYjGQlCLcUw4sF-TahirwKMLMt2VBfcCt2yg0=s2048?key=pFc4YR2jEYb-mSTWCPB1Vw)

## 6. Wait for the deposit and fund your Trading Account

After the bridge transaction is submitted, wait for the balance to arrive in Ekiden. Once it is credited:

1. Open your account balances.
2. Move the amount you intend to use for margin from your **Funding Account** to your **Trading Account**.
3. Confirm the transfer before placing an order.

<Info>
  The EVM to Canton Bridge is one available funding route. Use the deposit method and assets currently offered in the app for your account.
</Info>

## Funding Account and Trading Account

| Account | What it is used for |
| --- | --- |
| **Funding Account** | Holds assets after they arrive on Ekiden. |
| **Trading Account** | Holds the margin available for your positions and orders. |

## 7. Start trading

Once the Trading Account is funded, open the [Trading Guide](/trade) to choose a market, set leverage, and place an order.

<Tip>
  Start with an amount you are comfortable using. Perpetual futures can use leverage and involve risk.
</Tip>

## Need help?

For access issues, provide your Party ID and a screenshot of the screen you see to your Ekiden contact. For deposit or bridge issues, include the transaction status or transaction reference. For product issues, use **Share feedback** in the app.

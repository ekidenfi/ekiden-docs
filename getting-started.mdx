Ekiden is an orderbook-based perpetual futures venue on Canton Network. This guide walks you through account creation, access activation, funding, and your first trade.

<Note>
  Ekiden is live on mainnet. Access may require a Mainnet invite code.
</Note>

## Before you begin

- Use the [Ekiden app](https://app.ekiden.fi) to create or sign in to your account.
- Have a Mainnet invite code ready if your access has not yet been activated. Read the [Referral Program](/untitled-page) to learn how invite codes work.
- Review [Fees](/fees) and the [Trading Guide](/trade) before funding your account.

## Create and activate your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ekiden">
    Go to [app.ekiden.fi](https://app.ekiden.fi) and select **Sign up** or **Sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    Follow the onboarding prompts to create or connect your Canton identity. Ekiden uses a **Party ID** to represent your account on Canton Network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate Mainnet access">
    If prompted, enter a Mainnet invite code. Once access is active, you can continue to account funding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your account">
    Deposit USDCx into your Funding Account, then transfer the amount you want to trade to your Trading Account. Use only assets you intend to trade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place your first order">
    Open the [Trading Guide](/trade), choose a market, set your order parameters, and submit an order. You can track positions, orders, and performance from the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key concepts

### Party ID

A Party ID is your Canton identity. It is used to associate your account, balances, and activity with your Ekiden profile. Ekiden operates a Canton participant node to support the account and settlement flows used by the platform.

### Funding Account and Trading Account

Your Funding Account holds deposited assets. Transfer assets to your Trading Account when you are ready to use them as collateral for trading. Transfer them back when you want to manage available balances.

### Market access and risk

Perpetual futures use leverage and carry risk. Review [order types and position management](/trade), [fees](/fees), and the [API Reference](/api-reference/introduction) if you trade programmatically.

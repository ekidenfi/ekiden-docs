XP is credited as you trade and hold positions. Trading XP appears in your account within about 30 seconds, while holding XP is credited every minute after a position becomes eligible.

## Four ways to earn

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Take liquidity">
    **10 XP per \$1 of taker fees paid**

    Every taker fill earns XP based on the fee you actually paid.

    ```text
    taker_xp = taker_fees_paid * 10
    ```
  </Card>

  <Card title="Make liquidity">
    **5 XP per \$1 of taker-equivalent fees**

    Maker fills earn XP based on what a taker would have paid for the same trade. You still earn when your maker fee is zero or receives a rebate.

    ```text
    maker_xp = taker_equivalent_fees * 5
    ```
  </Card>

  <Card title="Hold a position">
    **Up to 1 XP per \$1,000 held for one hour**

    Positions accrue XP every minute after they have been open for five minutes. The rate is adjusted by effective leverage.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Refer traders">
    **15% / 10% / 5% of the XP earned by your network**

    Referral XP is paid three levels deep and added on top of the trader's reward.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## What that looks like

A trader takes a \$5,000 position at a 0.1% taker fee, holds it for six hours at 3x effective leverage, then closes it.

```text
Taker fee     $5,000 * 0.1%             = $5.00
Trading XP    $5.00 * 10                 = 50 XP

Risk factor   3x leverage / 10           = 0.3
Holding XP    5 units * 6 hours * 0.3    = 9 XP

Total                                      59 XP
L1 referral   59 * 15%                  = 8.85 XP
Displayed referral XP                     8 XP
Fraction carried forward                0.85 XP
```

The trader keeps all 59 XP. The referral reward is added on top.

<Info>
  **Small amounts are never lost.** XP is displayed as a whole number, but fractional XP is tracked precisely. Any remainder carries forward until it adds up.
</Info>

## How holding XP works

Holding XP rewards both position size and the risk carried. Two positions with the same notional can earn different amounts when they use different amounts of account equity.

```text
effective_leverage = position_notional / account_equity
risk_factor = min(max(effective_leverage / 10, 0.1), 1)
holding_xp = (position_notional / 1,000) * hours_held * risk_factor
```

| Rule | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Scales with leverage** | Holding XP increases with effective leverage and reaches the full rate at 10x and above. |
| **Floor at low leverage** | Lower-leverage positions still earn at one tenth of the full rate. The risk factor never falls below `0.1`. |
| **Five-minute minimum** | A position starts earning once it is five minutes old. The first five minutes are not credited retroactively. |
| **Accrues every minute** | XP is credited while the position is open; you do not need to close it first. |
| **Adding resets nothing** | Adding to a position keeps its age. Fully closing and reopening starts a new five-minute clock. |

## Referrals pay three levels deep

When a referred trader earns XP, referral rewards travel up their chain for three levels.

If a trader you referred earns 100 XP:

| Level | Recipient | Relationship | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --: |
| Earner | Your referral | Keeps every point earned | **100 XP** |
| L1 | You | Referred the trader directly | **+15 XP** |
| L2 | Your referrer | One level further up | **+10 XP** |
| L3 | Their referrer | Final rewarded level | **+5 XP** |

<Info>
  **Nobody pays for referral XP.** It is added on top. The trader keeps all the XP they earned, and no referral reward is deducted from another user's balance.
</Info>

| Rule | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Every source counts** | Referral rewards apply to XP earned from trading, holding, and quests - not only fees. |
| **Bind once** | You can add one referrer after signing up. Once added, the referrer cannot be changed. Self-referrals are not allowed. |
| **No cap** | There is no limit on the number of traders you can refer or the amount of referral XP you can earn. |

## Multipliers

Some accounts receive an XP multiplier through the access code used to join or through a season conversion.

| Rule | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **The highest multiplier wins** | Multipliers do not stack. If an account has more than one, only the highest applies. |
| **Applies to your own trading** | The multiplier boosts XP earned from your own fills and positions. |
| **Your referrers benefit too** | Referral rewards are calculated after your multiplier is applied. |

## Quests

Quests award a fixed amount of XP for completing a specific target. A quest can require you to:

- Reach a trading-volume threshold.
- Place a set number of trades.
- Bring in your first active referral.

Daily quests reset at midnight UTC. One-off quests can be completed once.

Available quests and progress are shown on the quests page.

## Good to know

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **When XP appears** | Trading XP appears within about 30 seconds. Holding XP is credited every minute while an eligible position is open. |
| **Fees are the basis** | Trading XP is proportional to fees, not raw trading volume. |
| **XP does not go down** | Losing a trade does not reduce your XP balance. |
| **Market makers are excluded** | Designated market-maker and internal accounts do not earn XP. |
| **One account, one chain** | XP and referrals belong to the root account, not individual sub-accounts. |

Your XP total, referral link, and quest progress are available on the rewards page.
